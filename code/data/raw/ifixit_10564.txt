replace the HP DM4-3050US  Laptop screen

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/replace+the+HP+DM4-3050US++Laptop+screen/10564

Device category: HP Laptop

Difficulty: Moderate

Tools needed: Precision Utility Knife, Tweezers, iFixit Opening Picks set of 6, Phillips #1 Screwdriver



Step 1: Turn off computer and remove the battery in the back side

Step 2: Remove screw covers in both sides with the knife

Step 3: Screw cover removed

Step 4: Open screws for both sides

Step 5: Use guitar pick to unclip the screen bezel

Step 6: Use guitar pick to unclip the screen bezel

Step 7: Remove the bezel

Step 8: Remove 4 screws of screen holder

Step 9: Remove screen from back panel and lay it on the keyboard

Step 10: Use the tweezer  to remove the tape and then disconnect the cable

Step 11: Place the new screen on the keyboard and connect cable

Step 12: Place the screen into the back cover and install 4 screws. Install the bezel, battery, and then turn on computer ENJOY WITH YOUR NEW SCREEN. Go to website link at below to order the new screen http://www.laptopscreen.com/English/model/hp-compaq/PAVILION~DM4-3050US/
