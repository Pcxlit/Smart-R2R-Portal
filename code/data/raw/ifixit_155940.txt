Toshiba Satellite P755-EZ1511D Laptop Display Assembly Replacement

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Toshiba+Satellite+P755-EZ1511D+Laptop+Display+Assembly+Replacement/155940

Device category: Toshiba Satellite P755-EZ1511D Laptop

Difficulty: Moderate



Step 1: First flip the device over so that the back side is facing upwards

Step 2: Push the two sliders toward the edge of the laptop. Remove the battery.

Step 3: Open the lid.

Step 4: Remove the four screw covers in each of the four corners of the screen.

Step 5: Remove the 4 screws in each of the 4 corners.

Step 6: Take your prying tool and proceed to pry the screen cover off and set to the side.

Step 7: Disconnect the wire from the camera and the backside of the display assembly.

Step 8: Remove the 6 screws holding the display assembly and holding brackets together.
