Laptop Battery Replacement Guide (Sealed, non-removable battery models)

Symptom: Battery swelling (trackpad or keyboard bulging upward), battery
not holding charge, or the laptop only powers on when plugged in.

Safety warning: A swollen lithium battery is a fire risk. Do not puncture,
bend, or apply pressure to a swollen cell. Work in a well-ventilated area
and keep a fire extinguisher rated for electrical/chemical fires nearby.

Tools needed: Precision screwdriver set, plastic pry tools, isopropyl
alcohol for adhesive residue, replacement battery matched to the exact
model number.

Step 1: Power off fully and remove the bottom panel screws.

Step 2: Before touching the battery, discharge static by touching an
unpainted metal surface.

Step 3: Disconnect the battery connector from the motherboard first, before
attempting to remove the battery cells themselves.

Step 4: The battery is usually held down with adhesive strips, not screws.
Gently pull the designated adhesive tabs at a low angle -- do not pry
directly under a swollen cell.

Step 5: If a swollen cell will not release with adhesive tabs, stop and
consult a professional; do not force it with a pry tool.

Step 6: Install the new battery, reconnect the connector, and verify the
laptop boots and charges before closing the bottom panel.

Common mistake: Using a non-matched replacement battery (wrong model
number) can cause charging circuit errors even if it physically fits.
