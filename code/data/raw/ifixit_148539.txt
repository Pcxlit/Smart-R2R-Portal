Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen2 Laptop Battery Replacement

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Lenovo+ThinkPad+P1+Gen2+Laptop+Battery+Replacement/148539

Device category: Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen2 Laptop

Difficulty: Moderate

Tools needed: Phillips #0 Screwdriver, Flathead 3/32" or 2.5 mm Screwdriver



Step 1: Remove the 7 screws holding the base cover in place. Remove the base cover.

Step 2: Peel up the insulated sheet from the battery connector. Unplug the battery connector in the direction showed by the arrow.

Step 3: Remove the two M2 × L4 mm, flat-head screws. Remove the two M2 × L5 mm, flat-head, screws.

Step 4: Remove the battery.
