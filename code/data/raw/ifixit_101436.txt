Dell Adamo XPS Laptop Battery Replacement

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Dell+Adamo+XPS+Laptop+Battery+Replacement/101436

Device category: Dell Adamo XPS

Difficulty: Easy



Step 1: Turn off the laptop before doing anything to the laptop. Then turn the laptop around on its back.

Step 2: On the back of the computer there is a latch that you have to slide and hold in order to remove the battery. Then you have to push the computer battery towards the computer and rotate upwards. Finally lift the battery out of the computer.

Step 3: Align the battery with the battery slot before rotating it in place. There will be a snap. Be careful snapping the battery in place don't use force to out in place.
