Replacing the battery of HP Pavilion dv4-1120us laptop

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Replacing+the+battery+of+HP+Pavilion+dv4-1120us+laptop/135076

Device category: HP Pavilion dv4-1120us

Difficulty: Very easy



Step 1: Verify the laptop model. Remove the power cable. The battery only requires to move a latch to the left. With this, it comes out by itself.
