Dell Inspiron 17 Laptop Battery Replacement

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Dell+Inspiron+17+Laptop+Battery+Replacement/165199

Device category: Dell Laptop

Difficulty: Moderate

Tools needed: Phillips #0 Screwdriver



Step 1: Before starting, make sure the laptop is powered off and disconnected from any power sources.

Step 2: Remove the Screws of the Laptop with help screwdriver. There are 6 screws to remove. Make Sure to open the back of the laptop delicately.

Step 3: Carefully disconnect the old battery from the motherboard. Gently remove the old battery from its housing by removing plug of battery.

Step 4: Insert the new battery into the housing. Connect the new battery to the motherboard. Securely reattach the battery back cover.

Step 5: Plug in the laptop and charge it to Full Capacity. Power on the laptop and make sure it works with the new battery.
