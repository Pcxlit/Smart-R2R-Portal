IBM Thinkpad x41t Laptop Wifi Card Replacement

Source URL: https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/IBM+Thinkpad+x41t+Laptop+Wifi+Card+Replacement/25012

Device category: IBM Thinkpad x41t Laptop

Difficulty: Moderate

Tools needed: Phillips #00 Screwdriver



Step 1: Flip the laptop over and remove the battery Remove all highlighted screws with a Phillips head screwdriver

Step 2: Gently unconnect the grey and black wires

Step 3: Lift the wireless card and pull it out of the motherboard Replace the wifi card and retrace your steps to reassemble the laptop
